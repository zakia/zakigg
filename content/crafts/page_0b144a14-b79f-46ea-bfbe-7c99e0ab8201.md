## Typography

Good defaults let notes read naturally without requiring one-off classes on every element. The hierarchy should remain obvious when prose includes **bold text**, _italics_, [links](https://zaki.gg), and `inline code`.

> Typography should make structure visible without competing with the writing.

### Lists

- Unordered lists use theme-colored markers.
- Wrapped lines align with their text rather than the marker.
  
  - Nested lists indent consistently.
  - Their marker style remains distinct.

1. Ordered lists preserve meaningful sequence.
2. Spacing stays consistent with unordered lists.
3. Multiple paragraphs can still belong to one item.

## Wet food

- 1 container per day.
  
  - 1/2 container in the morning and 1/2 container at night
- There's a slow feeder I use but not required!
  
  - I will usually smear 1/2 of the container into the slow feeder using the spatula (see image)
  - and store the rest in the tupperware in the fridge

## Dry food

- auto-feeder is full and already on the schedule, just gotta plug it into usb - I also gave you an adapter if you need
- if you need to adjust anything int he auto feeder, just gotta hold the 🔒 symbol for a few seconds and it will unlock then you can keep pressing ⚙️ to rotate between different settings and arrows to adjust

## Toys/Treats

- There's a mouse in the bag that he will chase sometimes if it's out and about
- Otherwise just any wand toy will usually work
- There's a treat dispenser toy I added if you want to try it with Eevee and Mizu too. I just fill it with a few treats and you can adjust the opening size and let them smack it around to get treats. Wally doesn't always play with it but It's nice to fill up with som treats if I'm out all day (but he has friends now so might not even be needed)
